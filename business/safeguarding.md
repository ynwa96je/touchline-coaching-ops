# Safeguarding and data (John to confirm against his governing body's requirements)
- Background checks and safeguarding training for every coach.
- Written parental consent before using any child's name, image or video.
- Collect the minimum data; store it only in the booking system; delete when no longer needed.
- Never message a child directly; communicate through parents or guardians.
- Medical and emergency information available to the coach at every session.
